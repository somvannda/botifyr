How it works

1 · Pick a bot

Create a bot for the job, or drop several into a group chat that replies in turn.

2 · Give it work

Paste a link, a repo, or a task. It plans, operates software, and asks before risky steps.

3 · Get results

Files, summaries and changes come back — with an audit trail and your approval.
